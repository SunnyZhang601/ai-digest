**Andrej Karpathy (former OpenAI/Tesla, now independent AI researcher)**

Karpathy dusted off an April tweet because the gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is widening into a sharp funnel rather than two groups talking past each other. He breaks the population into four tiers: roughly 6B people (~75%) who have barely touched LLMs; 1-2B (~20%) casual free-tier users who see "derpy chatbots" and treat them like better search; about 20M people (0.2%) doing professional math and code work who watch agents complete weeks-long projects from a prompt; and roughly 5,000 people (0.00006%) with internal access to frontier systems, seeing swarms of thousands of agents collaborate over weeks on mega projects. He attributes the funnel to three forces: impact scales with ambition and problem horizon, the jaggedness of capability (peaks in digital, verifiable, economically valuable domains due to RL on verifiable rewards), and access tiers. His closing point: the public sees a derpy chatbot, most professionals see modest uplift, and a tiny sliver feels the vertigo of the curve going vertical, all at the same time.

He calls agent swarms possibly the ultimate form factor for agents inside workflows, relevant to math, cyber, bio, materials science, software engineering, and finance. He notes less than a fraction of a fraction of the population uses AI this way today, due to model capability relative to domain, cost, available harnesses, and frontier model availability. **No Priors: Frontier Chips for Frontier AI Labs, with Walter Goodwin, Founder/CEO of Fractile**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeCP4wCxkA

The Takeaway: The chip space has a "frontier model" equivalent, and it is a structural three-to-six-month speed advantage, not a bigger die.

Walter Goodwin is the founder and CEO of Fractile, a full-stack AI chip company he started in summer 2022 to build very fast inference chips for the world's largest models. His central bet is on speed, and specifically on memory bandwidth, because he believes the real payoff of fast inference is not a snappier chatbot but radically faster long-running agents. "The Snappier chatbot is kind of the faster horses of kind of fast inference," he says, invoking Henry Ford. The place speed moves the dial is in taking multi-trillion parameter models and running them at many thousands of tokens per second so that long-horizon agents finish in a fraction of the time.

Fractile started with an SRAM-based design, like Grok and Cerebras, but Goodwin says growing context lengths made that approach unscalable. The company pivoted to working closely with memory vendors to get extremely high bandwidth access to higher-capacity DRAM, aiming to unite the scalability of GPU-style memory with the speed of SRAM chips. The economics collapse down to cost per gigabyte of memory, so bandwidth to cheap DRAM is the whole game.

On market structure, Goodwin is blunt: most hyperscaler first-party chips look alike because they are built with a small number of ASIC houses like Broadcom, using the same HBM, the same tensor cores, the same TSMC packaging. He argues frontier labs cannot go all-in on proprietary silicon, because if a rival discovers a computational breakthrough that only works on their own chip, the all-in lab could die in the nine months before deploying enough of theirs. That asymmetry is why a third-party frontier chip player persists. He also predicts the industry will compress chip design cycles far faster than the ten years a top semi CEO told the host it would take to go from architect intent to a usable GDS2 file, suggesting people should "divide it by four."

**No Priors：Frontier Chips for Frontier AI Labs，嘉宾是 Fractile 创始人兼 CEO Walter Goodwin**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeCP4wCxkA

核心要点：芯片领域也有"前沿模型"的等价物，那就是结构性的三到六个月速度优势，而不是更大的芯片面积。

Walter Goodwin 是 Fractile 的创始人兼 CEO，这家全栈 AI 芯片公司成立于 2022 年夏天，目标是为世界上最大的模型打造极快的推理芯片。他的核心赌注是速度，具体来说是内存带宽，因为他相信快速推理真正的回报不是更灵敏的聊天机器人，而是大幅加速长时间运行的 agent。他借用 Henry Ford 的比喻说："更灵敏的聊天机器人，就是快速推理里的'更快的马'。"速度真正改变格局的地方，是让数万亿参数的模型以每秒数千 token 的速度运行，从而让长周期 agent 用一小部分时间完成任务。

Fractile 最初做的是基于 SRAM 的设计，类似 Grok 和 Cerebras，但 Goodwin 说 context length 不断增长让这条路无法扩展。公司随后转向与内存厂商紧密合作，为更大容量的 DRAM 实现极高带宽访问，目标是同时拿到 GPU 式内存的可扩展性和 SRAM 芯片的速度。推理的经济账最终归结为每 GB 内存的成本，所以通往廉价 DRAM 的带宽就是全部关键。

谈到市场结构，Goodwin 很直白：大多数 hyperscaler 的自研芯片看起来都差不多，因为它们都是和少数几家 ASIC 厂商（比如 Broadcom）合作，用同样的 HBM、同样的 tensor core、同样的 TSMC 封装。他认为前沿实验室不可能 all-in 自研芯片，因为如果竞争对手发现了只在自家芯片上奏效的计算突破，all-in 的那家可能在九个月内就撑不到部署足够芯片的那天。正是这种不对称性，让第三方前沿芯片玩家得以存在。他还预测，行业压缩芯片设计周期的速度会远快于某位顶级半导体 CEO 告诉主持人的十年，后者说的是从架构师意图到可用的 GDS2 文件所需的时间，Goodwin 的建议是"把它除以四"。
