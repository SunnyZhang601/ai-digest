**Aaron Levie (CEO of Box)**

Aaron Levie laid out a detailed thesis that coding is a window into the future of knowledge work: jobs move from doing research, typing and value creation manually to coordinating what agents do, and then to giving agents goals to optimize against as that coordination step itself becomes a bottleneck. He argues this will not stay confined to coding, because agents will use computers, leverage existing tools or write new ones, and write code on the fly to solve larger swaths of knowledge work, so manufacturing, architecture, life sciences, material sciences and cyber all eventually fold into being coding adjacent. The big hurdle is that much of this work remains non-verifiable, so humans stay more in the loop than in coding, but the handoff portion will keep growing as models get more capable. His bottom line: pay attention to coding and you will get a sense for where work is going.

Aaron Levie（Box CEO）给出了一套完整判断：coding 是观察大量知识工作未来的窗口。工作会先从亲手做研究、打字、创造价值，变成协调 agent 在做什么；等这个协调步骤本身也成为瓶颈，人就会改为给 agent 设定目标去优化，agent 则去解决越来越大的问题。他认为这不会停留在 coding，因为 agent 会用电脑、复用现有工具或写新工具、现场写代码来解决更大范围的知识工作，制造业、建筑、生命科学、材料科学、网络安全等等最终都会变成 coding 的邻近领域。最大的障碍是很多工作仍然不可验证，所以人在回路中的程度会比 coding 更高，但随着模型变强，可以交接出去的部分会越来越大。他的结论是：盯住 coding，你就能看出工作要往哪走。

**Training Data — "OpenAI's Noam Brown: Why Game Theory Didn't Lead to General Intelligence (Live at No Limit Finals)"**

The Takeaway: Game theory assumes everyone is rational, and that assumption is exactly why it fails at the thing it was supposed to be best at, which is understanding people.

Noam Brown, a research scientist at OpenAI who worked on poker AI, then CICERO for Diplomacy, and then the reasoning line that became o1, started out believing computational game theory was the path to general intelligence. He spent his whole PhD on poker bots, won the MIT PokerBots competition two years running in 2014 and 2015, and then aimed at what he thought was the hardest possible game: Diplomacy, a natural language game of negotiation, alliance and betrayal. The bots trained on game theory kept doing things like offering a penny in the ultimatum game, technically optimal and socially suicidal. "The problem with game theory is that it assumes that all the other players are rational, that it is common knowledge that all players are rational, and people are not actually rational." What actually worked was training on human data to imitate people as closely as possible, in other words building a model of human irrationality and then deploying it against real humans.

That realization landed as a crisis of faith around 2022, when he had to choose between staying with the field he was an expert in or pivoting to general purpose reasoning in RL. He chose the pivot and joined OpenAI, where people told him to just pre-train a bigger model and everything would work out, while he pointed out that closing the skill gap to competent Go play would take something like six orders of magnitude. The other half of the answer was thinking before acting: chain of thought plus RL on chain of thought, which unlocked scaling test time compute in a general purpose way. Today he uses "make me a poker bot" as his personal eval for every new model, and says Astra is roughly able to make money playing online poker but still cannot build a full-scale bot alone, because it over-optimizes CFR numerics that do not matter and fails to notice it is going the wrong direction. His most contrarian advice for a promising student right now is to drop out and join the labs, and he gives AI a 20% chance of beating the World Series of Poker within five years, mostly because a single agent can just shove every hand and let variance decide.

The Takeaway：博弈论假设所有人都是理性的，而这个假设恰恰是它在最该擅长的领域，也就是理解人，失败的原因。

Noam Brown 是 OpenAI 的研究科学家，先做扑克 AI，再做 Diplomacy 的 CICERO，然后是后来变成 o1 的 reasoning 路线。他最初相信计算博弈论是通往通用智能的路，整个博士期间都在做扑克 bot，2014 和 2015 连续两年拿下 MIT PokerBots 比赛冠军，之后瞄准了他认为最难的游戏：Diplomacy，一个涉及谈判、结盟和背叛的自然语言游戏。用博弈论训练的 bot 会做出在最后通牒博弈里只给对方一分钱这种事，技术上最优，社交上自杀。"博弈论的问题在于它假设其他所有玩家都是理性的，而且所有人都知道所有人都是理性的，但人其实并不理性。" 真正有效的是用人类数据训练，尽可能模仿人，也就是造一个 "人类非理性" 的模型，再拿去和真人对抗。

这个认知在 2022 年前后变成了一次信仰危机，他必须在留在自己专长的领域和转向 RL 通用推理之间做选择。他选择了转向并加入 OpenAI，当时还有人跟他说只要预训练一个更大的模型一切就都解决了，而他指出，要把能力差距补到能下好围棋，大概需要六个数量级。答案的另一半是 "行动前先思考"：chain of thought 加上对 chain of thought 做 RL，这让 test time compute 可以以通用方式扩展。现在他每出一个新模型就拿 "给我做个扑克 bot" 当自己的评测，并说 Astra 大致已经能在线上扑克赚钱，但仍然无法独立做出完整规模的 bot，因为它会过度优化根本不重要的 CFR 数值精度，却意识不到自己走错了方向。他对当下有潜力的学生最反共识的建议是：退学，现在就加入实验室。他还给了 AI 五年内在世界扑克大赛上击败人类 20% 的概率，主要因为单个 agent 可以每手全下，把胜负交给方差。
