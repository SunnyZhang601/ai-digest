**Box CEO Aaron Levie**

Aaron Levie argued that the vast majority of enterprise workflows have not actually had agents applied to them in any meaningful way, estimating the economy has this deployed at under 1 percent, with most companies at best running a single LLM pass inside a few automated systems. He said this is mostly a statement about AI capability itself, and that computer use, long-running agents, harness improvements and surrounding infrastructure are finally making complex workflows addressable. He predicted self-improving systems that raise execution rates across many instances, and agent swarms that let you throw compute at a problem, with the real shift being organizations reengineering workflows rather than users prompting agents, with big impact ahead in finance, life sciences and healthcare.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，绝大多数企业工作流其实还没有以任何有意义的方式用上 agent，他估计整个经济体的部署率不到 1%，多数公司最多是在少数自动化系统里跑一次 LLM 调用。他说这主要反映的是 AI 本身的能力水平，而 computer use、长时间运行的 agent、harness 改进和周边基础设施终于让复杂工作流变得可解。他预测会出现自我改进的系统，能在大量实例上提升执行成功率，以及 agent swarm 让你可以把算力直接投向一个问题，真正的转变是企业重新设计工作流，而不是用户去 prompt agent，金融、生命科学和医疗等领域会有巨大影响。
