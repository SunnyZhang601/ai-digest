**Peter Yang (AI tutorials and interviews)**

Peter Yang shared two clips from his conversation with Anthropic's Nate Parrott about how Claude designs. Parrott explained that Claude operates in a fresh context each time, so it believes its default aesthetic (serif text, off-white background, orange accents, edge bars on cards) is totally original, which is why Anthropic's Claude Design prompt explicitly tells the model it over-biases toward those fonts and colors and to avoid them unless asked. On prototyping, Parrott noted that ideas that once took a year of pre-production at The Browser Company (makers of Arc) can now be built in one to two days at Anthropic, but the cycle of getting it into users' hands and learning whether they'll use it has not been compressed.

Peter Yang 分享了他与 Anthropic 的 Nate Parrott 对话中的两段片段，聊的是 Claude 如何做设计。Parrott 解释说，Claude 每次都在全新的 context 中工作，所以它会以为自己默认的审美（衬线字体、米白背景、橙色点缀、卡片边缘的竖条）是完全原创的，这也是为什么 Anthropic 的 Claude Design prompt 会明确告诉模型它过度偏向这些字体和颜色，除非用户要求否则不要使用。关于原型设计，Parrott 提到，当年在 The Browser Company（Arc 浏览器团队）需要一年 pre-production 才能验证的想法，现在在 Anthropic 一两天就能做出来，但把产品交到用户手里、搞清楚他们到底会不会用，这个循环并没有被压缩。

**Guillermo Rauch (CEO, Vercel)**

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch introduced xeet, a hermetic, pixel-perfect X post renderer written in safe Rust and wasm with zero browser, network, filesystem, or system fonts, rendering a post to PNG in about 1ms. It reproduces Blink and HarfBuzz text shaping, 1/64px line breaking, RTL bidi, 28 Chirp font subsets, CJK/Math/Emoji fallbacks, and "Show more" rules, making it 100x to 1000x faster than booting a browser to screenshot, while being just as accurate. Rauch framed it as an "autorepo": an oracle boots Chrome to verify tens of thousands of text layout rules, and a CI canary watches the original's evolution and files signed PRs for agents to act on, built around what he calls "verification engineering." He also noted that AI models are getting really good at naming things, citing a CLI he built to diagnose system slowdowns that the model named hog.

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 发布了 xeet，一个用 safe Rust 和 wasm 写的封闭式、像素级精确的 X 帖子渲染器，不依赖浏览器、网络、文件系统或系统字体，大约 1ms 就能把一条帖子渲染成 PNG。它复现了 Blink 和 HarfBuzz 的文字排版、1/64px 换行、RTL 双向文本、28 个 Chirp 字体子集、CJK/数学/emoji fallback，以及 "Show more" 规则，比启动浏览器截图快 100 到 1000 倍，精度却一样。Rauch 把它定位成一个 "autorepo"：一个 oracle 启动 Chrome 来验证数万条文字排版规则，一个 CI canary 监视原始版本的演进并提交签名 PR 供 agent 执行，整套东西围绕他所说的 "verification engineering" 构建。他还提到 AI 模型现在起名越来越厉害，举例说他做了一个诊断系统变慢的 CLI，模型把它命名为 hog。
