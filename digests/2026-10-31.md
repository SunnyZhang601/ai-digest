**Box CEO Aaron Levie**

Aaron Levie offers a litmus test for whether you're pushing AI hard enough: if you could have done what you're doing before AI, you're likely not pushing it enough. Six months ago the best AI could do was reproduce work you'd already produce, only faster; now the output should dramatically exceed what was possible before. He lists what that looks like: prototypes that are not just functional but have meaningfully better features, architectures delivering performance gains you couldn't otherwise pursue, research consuming every fact across every dimension, and business processes that operate entirely differently and learn from past failures. His conclusion: we're entering a period where AI is smarter than us on most axes, intelligence remains jagged, but combined with human strengths the outcomes become qualitatively different.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 给了一个判断标准，用来检验你是否把 AI 用到位了：如果你现在做的事，在没有 AI 的时候也能做出来，那你大概率还没用够。半年前，AI 最多只能把你本来就会产出的东西做得快一点；现在，产出应当远远超出过去能达到的水平。他列举了具体表现：原型不只是能用，还要有实质性更好的功能；架构要能带来原本追不上的性能提升；研究要覆盖所有维度上的每一个事实；业务流程要以完全不同的方式运转，并从过去的失败中学习。他的结论是：我们正进入一个 AI 在多数维度上比人更聪明的时期，智能依然是不均衡的，但与人类擅长的部分结合后，结果会发生质变。

**Latent Space: Using Agents for Long-Horizon Financial Work — Aman Singh, Arca**

The Takeaway: The most interesting AI-native companies won't be SaaS vendors selling agents to firms, they'll be firms that rebuild the business around agents, so the agents, not the humans, drive the workflow.

Aman Singh leads the AI platform team at Arca, an AI-native wealth management firm that recently raised 64 million dollars. Arca handles financial advice, planning, tax, insurance, and estate work for clients ranging from people who just sold a business to tech executives planning early retirement to families buying a first home. The core design principle is unusual: clients never touch the AI directly. "The role of AI at Arca is not to replace the advisor. It is to replace all of the work around the advisor." What clients experience is a human advisor's empathy, backed by machinery that is thinking about their money around the clock.

That inversion shows up in how work gets routed. Singh's line is that "agents prompt people more than people prompt agents." A portfolio rebalance or a tax bill analysis can span ten days; agents do the analysis, pull in the right human at step seven when their input is actually needed, and hand over a reviewable plan. Agents cannot move money, liquidate assets, or contact clients directly, those are hard human-in-the-loop guardrails. Model choice is driven by internal benchmarks rather than public leaderboards, and Arca stays deliberately model-agnostic, having moved through Gemini, OpenAI, and now Claude as primary, with its own harness rather than a lab's managed agent product. Singh is blunt about why: "owning your intelligence" is what lets you build something tailored enough to actually change the business.

The safety architecture is worth stealing. Every database write gets a fast LLM verification pass. Inbound email, documents, and calls are screened for prompt injection at the edges. Anything leaving the system, even a calendar invite, faces deterministic PII checks plus a consensus of small models watching for data exfiltration. Sandboxed code is translated into a strict format and the original code discarded before it reaches the backend. Every action, human or agent, produces a change log, which gives auditability by default and doubles as a signal feed for the agent loop. Staff edits and cancellations each require a "why," and those traces feed a nightly meta-agent that proposes harness improvements, though a human engineer still approves prompt changes because, as Singh puts it, "agents will make really nasty prompt changes if you leave them to it."

**Latent Space：用 agent 做长周期金融工作 — Arca 的 Aman Singh**

The Takeaway：最有意思的 AI 原生公司不会是卖 agent 给金融机构的 SaaS 厂商，而是围绕 agent 重建整个业务的公司，让 agent 而不是人来驱动工作流。

Aman Singh 是 Arca 的 AI 平台团队负责人。Arca 是一家 AI 原生的财富管理公司，最近融资 6400 万美元，业务涵盖财务建议、财务规划、税务、保险和遗产规划，客户从刚卖掉公司的人，到规划提前退休的科技高管，再到买第一套房的普通家庭。它的核心设计原则很反直觉：客户从不直接接触 AI。"AI 在 Arca 的角色不是取代顾问，而是取代顾问周围的所有工作。"客户感受到的是人类顾问的同理心，背后则是一套 24 小时为客户的钱运转的系统。

这种反转体现在任务如何被分派上。Singh 的说法是"agent 给人发 prompt 的次数，比人给 agent 发 prompt 的次数更多"。一次组合再平衡或税单分析可能横跨十天：agent 做分析，在第七步真正需要某个人的输入时才把人拉进来，并交出一份可审阅的方案。agent 不能动钱、不能清算资产、不能直接联系客户，这些是硬性的人类在环护栏。模型选择由内部 benchmark 决定，而不是公开榜单，Arca 刻意保持 model-agnostic，从 Gemini 到 OpenAI 再到如今以 Claude 为主，并且用自研 harness 而不是某家 lab 的 managed agent 产品。Singh 说得很直白："拥有你自己的智能"，才能做出足够贴合业务、真正能改变业务的东西。

它的安全架构很值得借鉴。每一次数据库写入都会过一遍快速的 LLM 校验。进来的邮件、文档和电话在边缘就被筛查 prompt injection。任何离开系统的数据，哪怕只是一个日历邀请，都要经过确定性的 PII 检查和一组小模型的共识投票来防数据外泄。沙箱里的代码会被翻译成严格格式，原始代码被丢弃后才到达后端。每一个动作，无论人还是 agent，都会产生变更日志，这既默认提供了可审计性，又反过来成为 agent 循环的信号来源。员工的每次修改或取消都必须写明"为什么"，这些 trace 每晚喂给一个 meta agent 提出 harness 改进建议，但 prompt 变更仍需人类工程师批准，因为正如 Singh 所说："你要是放任 agent 自己改，它们会写出非常糟糕的 prompt。"
