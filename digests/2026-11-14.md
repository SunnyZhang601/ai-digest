**Box CEO Aaron Levie**

Aaron Levie argued that because models keep improving fastest at coding, tool use, and computer use, agents that write software or operate existing software will outpace automation in every other domain of knowledge work. Rather than only a small slice of work being automated, he expects more and more work to become "coding-shaped" to fit what agents are good at, pointing to recent models that generate animated videos, games, and 3D product models. His key reframe: models don't need to know everything, they need to know how to use or generate software effectively combined with deep domain expertise to make judgment calls inside workflows.

Aaron Levie 认为，由于模型在编程、工具调用和 computer use 上进步最快，能写软件或操作现有软件的 agent，其能力提升速度会超过其他所有知识工作领域。他判断不会只是少部分工作被自动化，而是越来越多的工作会"变成编程的形状"去适配 agent 的强项，并举了近期模型生成动画视频、游戏和 3D 产品模型的例子。他的核心重构是：模型不需要无所不知，而是需要懂得如何高效地使用或生成软件，再叠加深厚的领域专业知识，才能在工作流中做出正确判断。
