**Box CEO Aaron Levie**

Aaron Levie predicted cyber will be one of the biggest spaces for AI agents, noting that the attack surface expands 100-fold because agents can work through any crack to find what they want, but the defensive upside is equally large: agent swarms reviewing every line of code, testing exploit permutations, and researching incidents faster than humans. He stressed that cyber has always been a category where you can never hire enough people or clear the backlog of known risks, and agents finally offer far better coverage. Separately, he detailed Box's testing of Gemini 4 Argon on their Box AI Complex Work eval, reporting a 14-point jump over Gemini 3.1 Pro across all tests, with sample wins including financial services (+18), healthcare (+25), technology (+37), energy (+16), and consumer products (+33), and said it will be available in Box AI Studio shortly.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 预测，cyber 将成为 AI agent 最大的应用领域之一。他指出，由于 agent 能钻进任何裂缝找到想要的东西，攻击面会扩大 100 倍，但防守端的机会同样巨大：agent 集群可以审查每一行代码、测试各种漏洞组合、比人类更快地研究正在发生的安全事件。他强调 cyber 一直是一个你永远招不够人、也永远清不完已知风险积压的领域，而 agent 终于让覆盖度大幅提升。另外，他详细介绍了 Box 用 Box AI Complex Work eval 测试 Gemini 4 Argon 的结果：在所有测试中比 Gemini 3.1 Pro 提升了 14 个百分点，分行业看金融服务 +18、医疗 +25、科技 +37、能源 +16、消费品 +33，并称该模型很快会在 Box AI Studio 上线。
