**Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch**

**Box CEO Aaron Levie**

Aaron Levie agreed that as agents take on long-running project work and swarms of agents parallelize toward a goal, the agent harness and orchestration architecture must evolve. He flagged the hardest part: how agents collectively share knowledge, track tasks, and coordinate with humans, getting resource access right without over- or under-exposing it, all while agents become a bigger security risk vector. He sees big opportunity for both horizontal and vertical approaches.

Aaron Levie 认同，随着 agent 承担越来越多长周期项目工作、并且有成群的 agent 并行推进同一个目标，agent harness 和编排架构必须演进。他指出最难的部分在于：agent 之间如何共享知识、跟踪任务、与人类用户协作，如何在不过度暴露也不暴露不足的前提下拿到所需资源，同时 agent 本身正成为更大的安全风险载体。他认为横向和纵向两种路径都有很大机会。

**OpenAI CEO Sam Altman**

Sam Altman laid out a concrete governance proposal: frontier model developers should jointly establish shared norms for evaluating models and red-teaming safety cases before first use, including shared transparency standards. He further proposed common limits based on perplexity and other measurements that developers commit not to cross until our ability to understand and monitor AI behavior hits a concrete milestone, such as a successor to CoT monitors, effectively an industry standard for a first development limit and what must be achieved before going beyond it. He framed this as building on the White House Accords commitment to standards and best practices.

Sam Altman 提出了一个具体的治理方案：前沿模型开发者应该共同建立共享规范，在模型首次使用之前就如何评估模型、如何对安全案例做 red-team 达成一致，其中也包括共享的透明度标准。他进一步提议设定基于 perplexity 等测量指标的共同上限，开发者承诺在理解与监控 AI 行为的能力达到某个具体里程碑（例如出现 CoT monitor 的继任者）之前不越过这条线，实质上就是为"第一道开发上限"以及"越过它之前必须达成什么"建立行业标准。他把这视为对白宫协议中关于标准和最佳实践承诺的延续。
