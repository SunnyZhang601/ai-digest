**Aaron Levie (CEO, Box)**

Aaron Levie 认为 AI 正在形成一种可预见的新形态：为特定任务或领域服务的自主系统，这远超 LLM 套壳时代。他给出三层理由：一是模型的工具与计算机使用能力提升，让 agent 能像人一样操作外部系统和软件，没有现成软件时还能自己写；二是模型在各垂直领域的判断力在进步，法律、生物研究、代码安全、供应链等场景都在变好；三是 sandbox、文件系统、数据管道等 harness 和周边基础设施成熟，支撑长时运行和超大规模数据处理。他预计这类系统会出现在 IT 自动化、网络运营、药物发现、制造业和 AI 研究等领域，几乎经济中每个角色都可能配一个甚至多个。

Aaron Levie argues a new form factor is emerging for AI: autonomous systems dedicated to specific tasks or domains, a huge leap past the LLM wrapper days. He gives three reasons. First, better tool and computer use lets agents operate external data systems and software much like a person would, and build software themselves where none exists. Second, model judgment is improving across verticals like legal, bio research, code security, and supply chain. Third, harnesses and surrounding infra such as sandboxes, file systems, and data pipelines now support long-running, arbitrarily complex work at enormous scale. He expects these systems across IT automation, cyber operations, drug discovery, manufacturing, and AI research, with one or more for nearly every role in the economy.
