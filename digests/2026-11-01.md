### Andrej Karpathy (AI researcher, former Tesla AI director and OpenAI founding member)

Andrej Karpathy shipped a weekend hack called HackerTube: annoyed by YouTube's recommendations, he had Claude filter 43 million comments spanning 20 years of Hacker News down to 6,454 videos that keep getting recommended, built in roughly two hours with Opus 5.5. The result gives excellent recommendations without any logins (state lives in the local browser session), uses a representative mid-video frame as the thumbnail instead of hyper-optimized uploads, and ships the underlying comment analysis as a zip file you can point your own Claude at. He notes the only manual work was creating a Google Cloud API key, booting a VPS, and buying a domain; once the box was up he gave Claude the IP and told it to update, secure, harden, deploy and optimize the code, and it did the whole thing. His broader point: many projects that would have taken weeks or months can now be few-shotted for breakfast.

Andrej Karpathy 做了一个周末项目 HackerTube：他被 YouTube 的推荐烦到了，于是让 Claude 过滤了 Hacker News 20 年间 4300 万条评论，筛出 6454 个被反复推荐的视频，用 Opus 5.5 大约两小时完成。结果是无需登录就能获得极佳的推荐（状态保存在本地浏览器 session 里），缩略图用视频中段的代表帧，而不是经过极致 A/B 测试优化的上传图，并且把底层的评论分析打包成一个 zip 文件，你可以让自己的 Claude 基于它做一版自己的。他说唯一的手动工作是创建 Google Cloud API key、开一台 VPS、买个域名；机器起来后他把 IP 给 Claude，让它更新、加固、部署并优化代码，Claude 全做完了。他更大的观点是：很多原本要花几周甚至几个月的项目，现在一个早餐的时间就能 few-shot 出来。

### Box CEO Aaron Levie

Aaron Levie argued that managing agents will become the new job description for many roles, not just coding: we are moving from a world where agents help us do our work incrementally better or faster, to us helping agents do their work. That turns each of these roles into a manager of a team of agents rather than an individual contributor with some AI leverage, with the biggest impact in information-heavy fields like coding, cybersecurity, legal work, biotech and engineering. The deeper shift is that leverage decouples from hours worked: for most of knowledge work history the only variable was hours (assuming fixed talent, coordination and strategy), but agents can now parallelize work to execute as much as needed, so people and organizations that reorient their operations around this sooner will see compounding gains.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，「管理 agent」会成为很多岗位的新工作描述，不只是写代码。我们正在从一个 agent 帮我们把工作做得更好更快的世界，转向我们帮 agent 完成它们工作的世界。这会把这类岗位从「带一点 AI 杠杆的个人贡献者」变成「一个 agent 团队的管理者」，影响最大的会是信息密集的领域，比如编程、网络安全、法务、生物科技和工程。更深层的变化是杠杆与工时脱钩：知识工作的历史上，主要可调的变量一直是投入的小时数（假设天赋、协作和战略固定），而现在 agent 可以替我们并行执行，理论上需要多少就能跑多少，所以更早发现这一点并据此重构运营的个人和组织，会在工作流上获得复利式收益。

### Unsupervised Learning — Ep 95: Modal CEO on the Future of Inference, the Chaos of the GPU Market & Origins of the Sandbox Bet

The Takeaway: The durable moat in AI infrastructure is not faster inference, it is being the multi-product platform that absorbs the chaos of the compute market so customers never have to think about it.

Erik Bernhardsson, founder and CEO of Modal, built the company on a contrarian bet: do the hard, unglamorous work of writing your own file system, container runtime and scheduler instead of leaning on Kubernetes and Docker. That bet took years longer than the easy path but now pays off in cold starts measured in seconds and the ability to snapshot GPUs and stream data over RDMA between nodes. Modal aggregates capacity across 25 different clouds, cutting about two or three compute deals a week, and Bernhardsson is blunt about how primitive that market still is.

"I went to GTC last year... And it was like an open air drug market, but, like, GPUs. Like, it's just like walking around, like, talking to people, trying to get GPUs."

His structural argument is that compute will stay unwieldy because it is not a fungible contract: reliability, location, networking and disk all vary, and some suppliers are former crypto miners who never planned for 24/7 operations. The fix is abstraction, taking failing capacity out of rotation automatically so the customer never notices.

The sandbox product is the proof of the asymmetric bet thesis: a prototype took a few days in 2023, sat quiet, then exploded in 2025 when models got good at code, forcing five rewrites and now running at a $300M run rate, about a third of revenue. LLM inference is another third, a business Modal deliberately entered after the DeepSeek moment convinced Bernhardsson the open source models were genuinely good. He is skeptical that raw token serving stays profitable, since routers like OpenRouter commoditize providers, and thinks the real differentiation is the portfolio: sandboxes, training, batch, fine-tuning and inference in one place, because customers do not want single-trick ponies.

On building, he says roughly 95% of merged lines of code at Modal are now written by agents, but humans still supervise closely because infrastructure carries tail risks. Counterintuitively, senior engineers adopted agents fastest, since it is like handing them ten interns and removing the typing-speed bottleneck. Agents also changed deprecation policy: because models trained on old code keep generating outdated API calls, features cannot simply be removed from docs and forgotten, and "agent optimization" now resembles SEO.

https://www.youtube.com/@RedpointAI

要点：AI 基础设施真正持久的护城河不是更快的推理，而是做一个多产品平台，把算力市场的混乱吸收掉，让客户根本不需要去想它。

Modal 创始人兼 CEO Erik Bernhardsson 押的是一个反直觉的注：不去用 Kubernetes 和 Docker，而是自己写文件系统、容器 runtime 和调度器，做那些艰难又不性感的工作。这条路比捷径多花了好几年，但现在换来的是秒级的 cold start，以及能给 GPU 做快照、在节点之间用 RDMA 点对点传数据的能力。Modal 在 25 家不同的云上聚合容量，每周大约谈两三笔算力交易，而 Bernhardsson 对这个市场有多原始毫不客气。

「我去年去了 GTC……那就像一个露天的 GPU 毒品市场。就是到处走，跟人聊，想办法搞到 GPU。」

他的结构性判断是：算力会长期保持这种难以驾驭的状态，因为它不是可互换的合约，可靠性、位置、网络和磁盘都各不相同，而且有些供应商是前加密货币矿工，从来没想过要做 7×24 的运营。解法是抽象化，把出故障的容量自动移出轮换，让客户毫无感知。

Sandbox 产品正是「非对称下注」的证明：2023 年原型几天就做出来了，之后安静了很久，2025 年模型写代码变强后突然爆发，逼着他们重写了五次，现在是一个 3 亿美元 run rate 的业务，约占收入的三分之一。LLM 推理是另外三分之一，这块业务是 DeepSeek 时刻让 Bernhardsson 相信开源模型真的行了之后才决定进场的。他怀疑单纯卖 token 能否长期赚钱，因为 OpenRouter 这类路由层正在把供应商商品化，他认为真正的差异化在于产品组合：sandbox、训练、batch、fine-tuning 和推理放在一起，因为客户不想要只会一招的工具。

关于研发方式，他说 Modal 现在合并的代码里大约 95% 由 agent 写成，但人类仍然要紧密监督，因为基础设施有大量尾部风险。反直觉的是，采用 agent 最快的是资深工程师，因为这相当于给他们配了十个实习生，解除了打字速度这个瓶颈。Agent 还改变了废弃策略：由于模型在旧代码上训练，会不断生成过时的 API 调用，功能不能只是从文档里删掉然后等三个月，而现在所谓的「agent optimization」已经有点像 SEO 了。
