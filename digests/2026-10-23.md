**Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch**

Guillermo Rauch weighed in on OpenAI's reported finding of a cross-VM timing attack, describing it as a "noisy neighbor" attack where one agent on a shared host can leak information through CPU cache timing. He noted this class of attack has been known since at least 2015 with no full solution, and that agents are exactly the kind of tireless, ingenious adversary that makes "theoretically possible" attacks real. The only complete fix is dedicated hardware per agent, which carries major economic costs; for Vercel Sandbox he says every practical mitigation is deployed, plus always-on monitoring and red-teaming.

Guillermo Rauch 评论了 OpenAI 据称发现的跨 VM 计时攻击，称其为"吵闹邻居"攻击：同一台共享主机上的一个 agent 可以通过 CPU 缓存计时泄露信息。他指出这类攻击至少从 2015 年起就为人所知，至今没有完整解法，而 agent 恰恰是那种不知疲倦、足够聪明的对手，能把"理论上可能"的攻击变成现实。唯一彻底的解法是每个 agent 独占硬件，但经济代价巨大；他表示 Vercel Sandbox 已部署所有可行的缓解措施，并配合全天候监控和红队测试。

**No Priors — Democratizing Security for the Agentic Web with Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht**

The Takeaway: AI has triggered a vulnerability apocalypse, but the same technology that caused it is the fastest path out, and the fix is more red teams, not fewer features.

Dane Knecht, CTO of Cloudflare, has spent the past year watching what felt like ten years of change compressed into one, and his central observation is that security has flipped from an afterthought to the main event. Where the industry used to see a Log4j-scale incident roughly once a year, serious vulnerabilities now surface weekly. His forecast is blunt: within eighteen months, more than half of engineering time will go to security. The attackers have changed too. Some of the biggest recent finds were not nation states but high schoolers pointing agents at targets the way they once played capture-the-flag, chasing bug bounties. "We're gonna call them agent kitties now," his host jokes, and Knecht agrees.

He is less doomsday than many of the technical people he talks to. Zero-trust architecture has already put many companies in a better position than the old castle-and-moat model, and when critical moments arrive, competitors stop being competitors. The uncomfortable part is that AI itself is currently making software less secure, not more: "AI, at least the early AI, is very verbose. It likes to create complexity." Human judgment still has to enforce simplicity and reduce attack surface. Cloudflare's answer is process plus pressure, codifying its engineering principles into a document called the Codex that agents can follow in every review, and scaling from one internal red team to four or five.

On the agentic web, Knecht expects a split between a human web and a machine web, with agents paying per-read microtransactions at something like a site's CPM rate, enabled by new payment rails. Cloudflare has shipped a monetization gateway with agent wallets and a stablecoin, plus a project with OpenAI called Signals to help agents discover new content instead of re-crawling everything. On tokens, he thinks it is silly to ask humans to pick a model per task and expects intelligent routing to take over. Cloudflare deliberately runs no token leaderboard, grading people on what they ship, with gentle emails only at extreme usage.

His most contrarian operational bet: hire a thousand interns at a 5,000-person company. The AI-native interns use the tools better than their managers, and managers who supervised them adopted AI faster.

https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht 的核心判断是：AI 引发了一场漏洞大爆发，但同一项技术也是最快的出路，解法是更多的红队，而不是更少的功能。

过去一年对他来说像是十年被压缩成一年，而他最核心的观察是：安全从"事后才想的事"变成了主线。以前行业大约一年遇到一次 Log4j 级别的重大事故，现在严重漏洞每周都在出现。他的预测很直接：18 个月内，超过一半的工程时间会花在安全上。攻击者结构也变了，最近一些最大的漏洞不是国家行为体发现的，而是高中生像过去打 CTF 一样把 agent 指向目标、冲着漏洞赏金去的。主持人开玩笑说"那我们现在叫他们 agent kitties"，Knecht 也认同。

他比很多技术圈的人更不悲观。零信任架构已经让不少公司比过去的"城堡加护城河"模式更有优势，而且真到危急时刻，竞争对手就不再是竞争对手。让人不舒服的一点是，AI 目前在让软件变得更不安全，而不是更安全："AI，至少早期的 AI，非常啰嗦，它喜欢制造复杂性。"简化设计、缩小攻击面仍然要靠人的判断。Cloudflare 的做法是流程加压：把工程原则写成一份叫 Codex 的文档，让 agent 在每次 review 中遵循，同时把内部红队从一个扩到四五个。

关于 agentic web，Knecht 预计会分化出人类网络和机器网络，agent 按类似网站 CPM 的价格做按次微支付，前提是新的支付通道。Cloudflare 已推出带 agent 钱包和稳定币的变现网关，并与 OpenAI 合作 Signals 项目，帮助 agent 发现新内容而不是反复爬全站。关于 token，他认为让人来为每个任务挑模型很傻，智能路由会接管。Cloudflare 刻意不搞 token 排行榜，只看交付成果，只有在用量极端时才发提醒邮件。

他最大胆的组织赌注是：在一家 5000 人的公司招 1000 名实习生。AI 原生的实习生比管理者更会用工具，而带过他们的管理者采用 AI 的速度也更快。
