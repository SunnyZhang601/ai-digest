## PODCASTS

**Latent Space: Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend**

The Takeaway: In a market full of bigger, older competitors, the winning edge is caring about the developer persona that everyone else ignored, and then doing the unglamorous infrastructure work nobody wants to touch.

Zeno Rocha went from making the Dracula editor theme and being one of GitHub's most active open source developers to founding Resend, an email infrastructure company now powering 4.3 million users and 120,000 paid customers, including Warner Brothers and HBO. He had zero email or infrastructure background, which he calls the best thing that ever happened: "there's no preconception of what is right, what is wrong." Where incumbents made new users fill out forms and wait days for human verification, Resend trusted users by default and put the burden on itself, blocking fast only when bounce and complaint signals go bad. Most of the time it's misuse, not abuse, a distinction he calls a skill issue.

The technical depth is the interesting part. Landing in the inbox at high volume is not a scaling problem you solve with more compute; Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo each behave differently, and you have to reverse engineer how phishing works and do the opposite. That means queuing the most engaged recipients first, spacing out batches across providers, buying and warming IP blocks, and keeping link and image URLs on the sending domain so a noisy neighbor can't get you blacklisted. He notes that auto-generating a plain-text version of every email boosted deliverability a lot, because spam filters can read it directly.

On agents, Resend's MCP server is growing at least 2x month over month, reaching over a million calls, and now exposes around 80 tools versus the handful it launched with, against the consensus view that fewer tools is better. "I want my product to be formless," he says, which is why the company has been API-first from day one. Getting there meant rebuilding the permissions model with OAuth and RBAC first. He also bets on Domain Connect, an obscure "OAuth for domains" protocol that turns DNS setup into a few clicks, because DNS is still the single biggest source of support tickets, especially from a new wave of AI-assisted builders who don't know what a CNAME is.

The most memorable line is about what success is actually for: "I would rather live in that reality than live in a reality where we're a hundred million dollar company and my daughter doesn't wanna play with me."

Latent Space：Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend

The Takeaway：在一个挤满更大、更老牌对手的市场里，胜出的关键是去在意那些被所有人忽视的开发者群体，然后去做那些没人愿意碰、也不光鲜的 infra 工作。

Zeno Rocha 从做 Dracula 编辑器主题、成为 GitHub 上最活跃的开源开发者之一，到创立邮件基础设施公司 Resend，如今服务 430 万用户和 12 万付费客户，客户包括 Warner Brothers 和 HBO。他完全没有邮件或 infra 背景，而他称这是发生过的最好的事："你不会带着什么是对、什么是错的先入之见。" 当老牌厂商让新用户填表、等上几天人工审核时，Resend 默认信任用户，把负担放在自己身上，只在 bounce 和投诉信号变差时快速封禁。大多数情况是误用而非滥用，他把这个区分称为 skill issue。

技术深度才是最有意思的部分。高量送达收件箱不是一个靠加算力就能解决的扩容问题；Gmail、Outlook 和 Yahoo 的行为各不相同，你得逆向工程钓鱼是怎么做的，然后反其道而行。这意味着把最活跃的收件人排在队列前面、在不同服务商之间错开批次、购买并预热 IP 段，以及让链接和图片 URL 都留在发信域名上，避免被"吵闹邻居"拖进黑名单。他指出，为每封邮件自动生成纯文本版本大幅提升了送达率，因为垃圾邮件过滤器可以直接读取内容。

在 agent 方面，Resend 的 MCP server 每月至少增长 2 倍，调用量已超过一百万，工具数量从最初几个增加到约 80 个，这与"工具越少越好"的主流观点相反。"我希望我的产品是无形的，"他说，这也是公司从第一天起就 API first 的原因。为此他们先重建了基于 OAuth 和 RBAC 的权限模型。他还押注 Domain Connect，一个冷门的"域名版 OAuth"协议，把 DNS 配置变成几次点击，因为 DNS 仍是支持工单的最大来源，尤其是来自一批不懂 CNAME 是什么的新 AI 辅助开发者。

最令人印象深刻的一句话是关于成功到底为了什么："比起成为一家一亿美元的公司、但女儿不想跟我玩，我更愿意活在前一种现实里。"
