**Latent Space — Scaling Linear's GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x0VSm6IjcDQ

The Takeaway: The most durable growth motion in AI-era software is not chasing the new category but deliberately sequencing it, and Linear's COO deliberately crossed out "go enterprise" from the company goals three weeks into the job because the team had exactly one salesperson.

Cristina Cordova is COO of Linear, which she joined about three and a half years ago after leading platform, partnerships and self-serve growth at Notion and spending seven and a half years at Stripe on early partnerships and go-to-market. She is unusually candid about what it actually takes to move a product-led, startup-loved tool upmarket, and the answer is mostly patience and sequencing rather than ambition.

Her most counterintuitive move: at her first company offsite, in front of roughly 40 people, she showed the three company goals and crossed out the one that said "move up market to enterprise." Her reasoning was blunt: "We have one person. We have literally one person on sales. You're not going upmarket to enterprise with one person." She replaced it with a goal of winning growth-segment companies away from incumbents, and only in 2025 did she stand up and announce the enterprise push.

The strategic insight underneath is about switching costs. You cannot be 20% or 30% better and expect companies to rip out an existing system; the advantage has to be significant. Linear's founders had an edge because they had used the legacy tools at Coinbase, Airbnb and Uber and knew "no one liked these tools." The wedge was design and UX, delivered through a free product people could try before they believed.

On AI, Cordova is refreshingly specific and contrarian about the "just be a database with an MCP server" strategy: "I think you have to bring the agentic workflows into your tools, and you have to make them better than the tools that people would be using externally to interoperate with your system. Otherwise, there's no reason for people to go into your tool." The numbers back her up: over 50% of issues created on Linear are now created by agents, yet app usage is rising too, because chat is single-player and bad at visualizing a team's work.

She is also skeptical of AI-first support. Linear uses LLMs to draft replies but keeps humans as the quality check, because "I don't believe in blindly using these tools in a way that makes your brand compromised." The payoff shows up in customer surprise that a support interaction was fast and good, which she calls "kinda sad" for the rest of the market.

Cristina Cordova 是 Linear 的 COO，约三年半前加入，此前在 Notion 负责平台、合作与自助增长，并在 Stripe 工作了七年半，负责早期合作与 go-to-market。她非常坦诚地讲了把一个产品驱动、受创业公司喜爱的工具推向更高端市场到底需要什么，答案主要是耐心和节奏，而不是野心。

她最反直觉的举动是：在第一次公司 offsite 上，她当着大约 40 人的面展示了三个公司目标，然后把"进军企业市场"那一条划掉了。她的理由很直接："我们只有一个人。销售团队真的只有一个人。你不可能靠一个人打进企业市场。"她把它换成了"赢下成长期公司、从 incumbent 手里抢客户"的目标，直到 2025 年才正式宣布进军企业市场。

背后的战略洞察是关于切换成本。你不可能只比别人好 20% 或 30% 就让公司拆掉现有系统，优势必须足够显著。Linear 的创始人有天然优势，因为他们在 Coinbase、Airbnb 和 Uber 用过那些老工具，知道"没人喜欢这些工具"。切入点就是设计和 UX，通过免费产品让人先试再信。

关于 AI，Cordova 对"只做一个带 MCP server 的数据库"这一策略给出了少见而具体的反驳："你必须把 agentic workflow 带进你的工具里，而且必须做得比用户从外部接入你系统时用的工具更好。否则，人们就没有理由进入你的工具。"数据也支持她：现在 Linear 上超过 50% 的 issue 由 agent 创建，但 app 使用量同时也在上升，因为聊天是单人的，也不擅长可视化团队的工作。

她对 AI 优先的客服同样持怀疑态度。Linear 用 LLM 起草回复，但保留人类做质量把关，因为"我不相信盲目使用这些工具、以至于损害你的品牌"。回报体现在客户的惊讶上：一次客服交互又快又好，她说这对市场上其他产品来说"有点悲哀"。
