**Latent Space — Building Visual Intelligence — Andreas Blattmann & Dustin Podell, Black Forest Labs**

The Takeaway: Image generation was never the end goal. The same diffusion and flow-matching machinery that made Stable Diffusion work is, in Black Forest Labs' view, the foundation for a broader "visual intelligence" that learns physics and intuition from pixels, video, and eventually actions, and that is the road to robotics.

Andreas Blattmann, co-inventor of latent diffusion, and Dustin Podell, who went from freelance web developer to model and research lead behind SDXL, Flux.1, and Flux Context, trace a straight line from a university algorithm to a company that has raised roughly $450 million and crossed $100 million in revenue. Their framing is refreshingly specific: language models are closer to Kahneman's System 2, slow deliberate reasoning, while what they are building is System 1, the fast intuitive intelligence you use to catch a ball or grab a bottle without solving any equations. "We wanna be transforming that knowledge and that form of intelligence, which is very different from language model intelligence, into models." The bet is that you do not need an explicit 3D engine. In their Stable Video Diffusion work, a model pretrained only on plain 2D video was fine-tuned into a working 3D model in about 1,000 steps, evidence that 3D understanding is already latent in 2D data. The recurring lesson across their careers is that data, not algorithmic cleverness, is the dominant lever, and that the simplest scalable method usually wins. The SDXL refiner, a second full diffusion model bolted on to fix high-frequency detail, is a case study in the trade-off: it worked, but it was a pipeline patch rather than one model that does everything. That humility about complexity, and the willingness to extend rather than pivot, is what took them from an image lab to a bet on world models.

The Takeaway：图像生成从来不是终点。在 Black Forest Labs 看来，让 Stable Diffusion 奏效的那套 diffusion 和 flow matching 机制，是更广义"视觉智能"的基础，它从像素、视频乃至动作中学习物理和直觉，而这正是通向机器人之路。

latent diffusion 的共同发明人 Andreas Blattmann，以及从自由职业网页开发者一路做到 SDXL、Flux.1 和 Flux Context 模型与研究负责人的 Dustin Podell，串起了一条从大学算法到一家融资约 4.5 亿美元、收入已突破 1 亿美元的公司的直线。他们的框架非常具体：语言模型更接近 Kahneman 所说的 System 2，缓慢而深思的推理；而他们在构建的是 System 1，那种接球或抓瓶子时不需要解任何方程的快速直觉智能。"我们想把这部分知识和这种形式的智能，这种与语言模型智能非常不同的智能，转化进模型里。" 他们的赌注是：你不需要一个显式的 3D 引擎。在 Stable Video Diffusion 的工作中，一个只用普通 2D 视频预训练的模型，大约 1000 步 fine-tuning 就变成了可用的 3D 模型，证明 3D 理解已经潜藏在 2D 数据里。贯穿他们职业生涯的反复教训是：数据而非算法的巧妙才是主导杠杆，而最简单可扩展的方法通常胜出。SDXL 的 refiner，一个额外接上去修复高频细节的完整 diffusion 模型，正是这种权衡的案例：它有效，但那是 pipeline 补丁，而不是一个什么都能干的模型。这种对复杂度的谦逊，以及选择延伸而非转向的意愿，正是他们从图像实验室走向世界模型赌注的原因。
