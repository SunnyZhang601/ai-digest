**Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch** (rauchg on X)

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch laid out his thesis for what happens to frameworks and component libraries in the agents era: you have to "drill deeper" — delete the part, optimize, accelerate, automate. He argues a UI SDK is like the x86 instruction set for agents to spontaneously build new UI components, and that high-level, rigid, single-framework abstractions are "kryptonite" for an infinite, untiring creation machine. The promise: packaged behaviors that give accessibility, performance and cohesion while saving tokens and errors, with zero limitations on what UIs can be generated.

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 阐述了他对 agent 时代框架和组件库走向的判断：必须"往下钻"——删掉零件、优化、加速、自动化。他认为 UI SDK 就像给 agent 用的 x86 指令集，让 agent 能即兴构建新的 UI 组件；而那些高度抽象、僵化、绑定单一框架的东西，对一台无限且不知疲倦的创造机器来说就是"氪石"。他承诺的是：把可访问性、性能和一致性打包成行为，节省 token 和错误，同时对能生成什么样的 UI 毫无限制。

**No Priors — Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg**

The Takeaway: Drug discovery is still an empirical, trial-and-error craft, and the biggest win from AI isn't faster experiments in the real world, it's moving as much of the learning as possible onto a computer so that the real world is only used to validate.

Max Jaderberg is president and a founding team member of Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet-backed company with the stated goal of solving all disease. He came up through computer vision research in Oxford, sold a company to DeepMind in 2014, and spent years on AlphaGo and AlphaStar before concluding that the same techniques should be pointed at biology. His framing of the industry's problem is refreshingly blunt: new drug approvals per year have stayed roughly flat for decades even as the number of papers exploded 10 to 20x, the cost per drug climbed to about $1.5 billion, and timelines stretched to 15 years. The reason, he argues, is that medicine has always been made empirically, and the low-hanging fruit of that process is now exhausted.

The contrarian part is where he wants to spend effort. Rather than building ever-faster robotic labs, he wants to get out of the real world entirely. "The big time bottleneck, the big cost bottleneck is synthesizing real atoms," he says, and you eventually hit the laws of physics: a reaction takes as long as it takes, a cell culture takes the days it takes. So Isomorphic's internal leaderboards carry a dotted line labeled experimental accuracy, and the goal is simulation models good enough that you only enter the lab to validate, not to learn. He is equally skeptical of animal models, noting that a rat is only about 30 percent predictive of human clinical toxicity, and jokes that if one of his AI scientists pitched him a model that accurate he'd say "maybe do a bit better than that." Instead he wants in vitro endpoints, organoids and organs-on-a-chip combined with predictive models to directly model human toxicity.

On the business, Isomorphic keeps the drug design engine entirely in house and does not license it or sell tokens, using it both for its own pipeline and for partnerships with pharma companies that bring decade-old hard problems. Internal focus is oncology, immunology and inflammation, with an eye on root-node targets like KRAS. On timelines he declines to give a clinical trial date but offers a five-year test: he'd be "really disappointed" if predictive understanding of disease biology and the ability to design precise, low-toxicity molecules hadn't step-changed massively.

The Takeaway：药物研发至今仍是一门靠经验试错的工艺，AI 带来的最大胜利不是让真实世界的实验更快，而是把尽可能多的"学习"搬到计算机上，让真实世界只用来做验证。

Max Jaderberg 是 Isomorphic Labs 的总裁兼创始团队成员，这家由 Alphabet 支持的公司目标是"解决所有疾病"。他出身牛津的计算机视觉研究，2014 年把公司卖给了 DeepMind，之后多年投入 AlphaGo 和 AlphaStar，最终认定同样的技术应该用在生物学上。他对行业问题的概括相当直白：几十年来每年新药获批数量基本持平，而论文数量暴涨了 10 到 20 倍，单药成本涨到约 15 亿美元，周期拉长到 15 年。他认为原因在于制药一直靠经验驱动，而这条路上的低垂果实已经摘完了。

他真正想投入的地方则有些反直觉。与其造越来越快的机器人实验室，他想彻底走出真实世界。他说："最大的时间瓶颈、最大的成本瓶颈，是合成真实的原子。"而且你终究会撞上物理定律：一个反应该花多久就花多久，细胞培养该等几天就得等几天。所以 Isomorphic 内部的排行榜上都画着一条标着"实验准确度"的虚线，目标是让模拟模型好到进实验室只为验证，而不是为了学习。他对动物模型同样怀疑，指出大鼠对人间临床毒性的预测力只有约 30%，还开玩笑说，如果他的 AI 科学家拿这么一个模型来汇报，他会说"能不能再好一点"。他更想用体外指标、类器官和器官芯片，配合预测模型，直接对人类毒性建模。

商业上，Isomorphic 的药物设计引擎完全自研自用，既不授权也不卖 token，既用于自有管线，也用于和药企的合作，对方往往带来做了十年二十年都没进展的难题。内部聚焦肿瘤、免疫和炎症，关注 KRAS 这类"根节点"靶点。至于时间表，他不肯给临床试验的具体日期，但给了一个五年检验标准：如果对疾病生物学的预测性理解、以及设计精准低毒分子的能力没有发生巨大的跃迁，他会"非常失望"。
