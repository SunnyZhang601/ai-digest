**Amjad Masad (CEO, Replit)**
Amjad Masad shared new Replit research on scaling laws for agent swarms, with several counterintuitive findings: given a fixed two-hour window, Opus 5.5 went from a 3.7x speedup on NumPy with one agent to 18.9x with 32 agents; GPT-6.2 Astra was the strongest solo agent but showed negative scaling beyond 8 agents; models spontaneously developed different organizational structures, and decentralized swarms scaled better than centrally coordinated ones; problem parallelizability strongly predicted swarm performance; and individual model intelligence was a poor predictor of collective intelligence. Replit is open-sourcing Optimization-Bench to push research on this new dimension of inference-time scaling. Separately, he described a Friday meeting with VP JD Vance on American AI leadership, cybersecurity, and modernizing software infrastructure, stressing that allies wanting sovereign AI are increasingly looking to Chinese open models, and warning that America could win the frontier race yet lose the global AI ecosystem.

Amjad Masad 分享了 Replit 关于 agent swarm scaling law 的新研究，里面有几个反直觉的发现：在固定的两小时窗口内，Opus 5.5 在 NumPy 任务上从单 agent 的 3.7 倍加速提升到 32 个 agent 的 18.9 倍；GPT-6.2 Astra 是单 agent 最强，但超过 8 个 agent 后出现负向 scaling；模型会自发形成不同的组织结构，去中心化的 swarm 比依赖集中协调的扩展得更好；问题的可并行性是 swarm 表现的强预测指标；而单个模型的智能水平几乎无法预测集体智能。Replit 还开源了 Optimization-Bench，推动这个 inference-time scaling 新维度的研究。另外，他提到周五与副总统 JD Vance 会面，讨论了美国 AI 领导力、网络安全和软件基础设施现代化，并强调盟友想要主权 AI、正越来越倾向中国的开源模型，警告美国可能赢了前沿竞赛却输掉全球 AI 生态。
