**Aaron Levie** (CEO of Box)

Levie argues we're now seeing productivity gains "an order of magnitude or two above output levels pre-AI," and that this gap is not closing over time. The ingredients he lists: the right harness, deep domain understanding, usually a relatively new codebase, fluency with running multiple agents at once, and low-friction test-and-deploy. His counterintuitive point is that prior depth plus a willingness to work differently compounds leverage rather than converging it, and he expects the pattern to spread from software into science, AEC, finance, and cyber. In a second thread he argues AI inference is unusually latency-insensitive, so long-running agents can be dispatched to remote "AI factories," treated as ephemeral systems, and located almost anywhere, which is an economic opportunity for regions that court the buildout and a loss for those that don't.

Levie 认为，现在出现的生产力提升已经"比 AI 之前的产出水平高出一到两个数量级"，而且这个差距并没有随时间收窄。他列出的前提是：合适的 harness、对领域的深刻理解、通常是一个比较新的代码库、能同时驾驭多个 agent 的熟练度，以及摩擦很低的测试和部署流程。他最反直觉的观点是：过去的深度加上现在愿意换一种做法，会让杠杆不断复利而不是收敛，并且他认为这个模式会从软件扩散到科研、AEC、金融和网络安全。在另一条长帖里他指出，AI 推理对延迟异常不敏感，所以长时运行的 agent 可以派到远端的"AI 工厂"，这些设施可以被当作临时系统，选址几乎不受限制。这对积极招揽数据中心建设的地区是巨大的经济机会，对放弃这个机会的地区则是损失。

**Aditya Agarwal** (General Partner at South Park Commons, co-founder of Bevel Health, ex-CTO of Dropbox)

Agarwal draws an investing conclusion from the difficulty of building data centers: if capacity is hard to add, you should be "super long" anyone who already has it, meaning the hyperscalers. Demand for the "spice" only goes up, so the holders win big. What suffers is the rate of new model training, which leads him to a wry conclusion: "I guess we will pace the frontier after all. The body politic speaketh."

Agarwal 从数据中心建设变难这件事推出一个投资结论：如果新增产能很难，那就应该"超配"那些已经拥有产能的公司，也就是 hyperscalers。对"香料"的需求只会上升，所以持有者会大赢。受损的是新模型训练的速度，于是他给出一个略带自嘲的结论："看来我们终究会放慢前沿的步伐。民意如此。"

**No Priors: Tokenization, Agentic Trading, Harmonic: Robinhood's Mission to Expand Ownership with CEO Vlad Tenev**

The Takeaway: Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev thinks the biggest risk from AI isn't the technology itself but who gets to own it, and his answer is to push ownership down to everyone, from a $1,000 account for every newborn to tokenized private shares trading around the clock.

Tenev runs Robinhood, Harmonic, and a widening set of ownership products, and his framing is unusually coherent for a fintech CEO: if you sum up the company in one word, it's "ownership." That idea comes from a specific place. Born in Bulgaria in the late 80s, he watched 170% then 2,000% inflation, rolling blackouts, and a grandfather who converted every paycheck into copper cookware because it held value better than currency. "It was kind of the first real world asset that I encountered people investing in," he says.

That history shapes his read on AI. He finds the current wave of models solving math problems exciting but "too centralized," with labs running secret models in big data centers while the public watches. He argues the same discovery, say a partial Riemann hypothesis proof, would be far better received if it came from a random person using a tool than from a lab's internal run, and that the labs would have been wiser to collaborate with mathematicians rather than appear to be replacing them.

On product, the numbers are concrete: over 150,000 customers have opened agentic trading accounts, with roughly 30 million tool calls per day following a smooth exponential. Robinhood Agents now puts the whole harness inside the account, with skills, an app store, and loops you can schedule. His north star is putting hedge fund capabilities into normal people's pockets, and he expects it to follow the coding-agent curve: sophisticated users first, then everyone.

He's also blunt about why people resent AI data centers. If individuals don't own any of the upside, they only experience the noise and the job threat. Crypto, he notes, generated a political army despite plenty of people losing money, because ownership was distributed from day one. Hence Robinhood Ventures (a publicly traded VC fund with OpenAI exposure), a fund with YC for seed and Series A, and Trump accounts, where Robinhood is sole initial trustee, seeding $1,000 for every child born from January 2025.

这段经历塑造了他对 AI 的看法。他觉得当下这波用模型解数学题的浪潮很激动人心，但"太中心化"了：各大 lab 在大数据中心里跑着秘密模型，公众只能旁观。他认为同样的发现，比如黎曼猜想的部分证明，如果是一个普通人用工具做出来的，会比 lab 内部跑出来的结果更被接受；而 lab 们如果去找数学家合作，而不是显得在取代他们，会明智得多。

产品层面的数字很具体：超过 15 万客户开通了 agentic trading 账户，每天大约 3000 万次 tool call，呈平滑的指数增长。Robinhood Agents 现在把整套 harness 放进账户里，有 skills、有应用商店、还能设定时循环执行。他的北极星是把对冲基金的能力装进普通人的口袋，并预期它会沿着 coding agent 的曲线走：先是老练的用户，然后才是所有人。

他对人们为什么反感 AI 数据中心也说得很直白：如果个人享受不到任何收益，他们感受到的就只有噪音和饭碗威胁。他指出，crypto 尽管让不少人亏了钱，却催生出一支政治大军，因为所有权从一开始就是分散的。于是有了 Robinhood Ventures（一只公开交易的 VC 基金，持有 OpenAI 敞口）、一只与 YC 合作投 seed 和 Series A 的基金，以及 Trump accounts：Robinhood 是唯一的初始受托机构，为 2025 年 1 月起出生的每个孩子存入 1000 美元。
