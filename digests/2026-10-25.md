**Box CEO Aaron Levie (levie on X)**

Aaron Levie gave a detailed read on enterprise security: the risk surface is fragmenting and getting gnarlier, with endless libraries, tools, and sprawling infrastructure, data, and code to secure, and most large enterprises can barely keep up, let alone smaller companies. He argued the fix starts with modernizing tech stacks and consolidating fragmented data so you actually know your risk state, and only then can agent swarms fan out, use any security tool, test attack variations, read logs, and process enterprise data for risk. He called this a huge opportunity across every layer of the security stack, and a reason to be optimistic about the offense-defense balance, provided everyone does the hard work of getting the basics right first.

Aaron Levie 详细解读了企业安全的现状：风险面正在碎片化、越来越棘手，需要防护的库、工具、基础设施、数据和代码无穷无尽，大多数大型企业几乎跟不上，更别说小公司了。他认为解法首先要从现代化技术栈、整合碎片化的数据入手，让你真正掌握自己的风险状态，然后 agent 集群才能铺开，调用各种安全工具、测试各种攻击变体、读日志、处理企业数据来评估风险。他说这是安全栈每一层的巨大机会，也让人对攻防平衡保持乐观，前提是每个人都先把基础工作做扎实。
