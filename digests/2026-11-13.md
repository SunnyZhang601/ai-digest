**Training Data: A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning**

The Takeaway: Continual learning won't be won by a smarter base model alone, it will be won by whoever builds the boring, beautiful interface that turns a company's real-world traces into model improvements, and that interface is the missing layer between plentiful intelligence and plentiful experience.

Ronak, co-founder of Trajectory, spent years inside the places where this problem bites hardest: Windsurf, where the team trained a model on production edits, and before that DeepMind, where the entire business model was training one giant model and shipping it to everyone. That model, he argues, cannot serve the customer who wants a model customized for one firm. "If you want to basically take one model, customize that for a single person, and then distribute that one model, like just for that customer... that is an immense amount of infrastructure for a massive lab to build just for one customer."

His map of the field is refreshingly concrete. Today's agents are pseudo continual learners, like planning a road trip with AAA paper maps and having to call in for a route change. Trajectory's first product is MapQuest: democratize training so anyone can customize a model. The end state is Google Maps: every drive updates the map. The three ingredients are traces (capture everything in production, including sub-agents and user feedback), replayability (an environment that reproduces the trace, usually a Docker file), and the training stack itself, the loss and reward functions that labs guard closely. Trajectory abstracts the third away and attacks the first two with research.

Two results stand out. With Harvey, they trained a legal model that went from roughly 4% accuracy on an open source base to about 18%, at six times cheaper than frontier, freeing up far more tokens per task. With Rogo, they optimized for what Rogo calls intelligence density: same task, fewer tokens, cheaper and faster. On the research side, they pushed GRPO toward "group size one," learning from single rollouts across 10,000 different tasks instead of replaying the same environment 16 times, which removes the need for a perfect simulated environment. For reward, they select 100 representative traces from 10,000, have the customer label those densely, then train a meta grader to propagate the judgment across the rest.

The most interesting thread is on curiosity and alignment. Ronak contrasts recursive self improvement, a model in a black box optimizing itself, with continual learning, which learns from interacting with humans. "I don't want to end up in a world where these agents are going rogue, I think the way to do that is to keep on this alignment path." His framing of what's missing: LLMs jump from state zero to state one as next-token predictors, but they have no representation of the transition function, the thing a child learns by throwing a football a hundred times. Quantify that gap and you get curiosity. And his view of the product builder's future is lovely: as harnesses become most of the product, builders become gardeners, setting trellises and letting the plant grow, imbuing taste rather than writing code.

**Training Data：A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning**

核心要点：continual learning 的胜负不会只取决于基座模型更聪明，而取决于谁能做出那个枯燥但漂亮的界面，把公司真实世界里的 trace 转化成模型改进，而这个界面正是"充沛的智能"与"充沛的经验"之间缺失的那一层。

Trajectory 联合创始人 Ronak 常年在问题最痛的地方工作：在 Windsurf，团队用生产环境中的编辑数据训练模型；更早在 DeepMind，整个商业模式就是训练一个巨型模型然后分发给全世界。他认为这个模式无法服务那种想要为单一机构定制模型的客户。"如果你想拿一个模型，为某一个人定制，然后只服务那一个客户去分发……对一家大实验室来说，只为单个客户就要建起极其庞大的基础设施。"

他对行业的描绘非常具体。今天的 agent 只是伪 continual learning，就像用 AAA 的纸质地图规划公路旅行，想改路线还得打电话或跑一趟门店。Trajectory 的第一代产品是 MapQuest：让训练民主化，人人都能定制模型。终局是 Google Maps：每一次驾驶都在更新地图。三个要素是 trace（把生产环境中的一切记录下来，包括 sub-agent 和用户反馈）、可回放性（能复现 trace 的环境，通常是一个 Docker file），以及训练栈本身，也就是实验室守得很紧的 loss 和 reward 函数。Trajectory 把第三块抽象掉，用研究去攻前两块。

有两个结果值得注意。和 Harvey 合作，他们训练出的法律模型在开源基座上从约 4% 准确率提升到约 18%，成本比 frontier 便宜六倍，于是每个任务能花更多 token。和 Rogo 合作，他们优化的是 Rogo 所说的 intelligence density：同样的任务，更少的 token，更便宜更快。研究侧，他们把 GRPO 推向"group size one"，从 10000 个不同任务的单次 rollout 中学习，而不是把同一环境重放 16 次，从而不再需要完美的模拟环境。reward 方面，他们从 10000 条 trace 中挑出 100 条有代表性的，让客户密集标注，再训练一个 meta grader 把判断传播到其余数据。

最有意思的是关于好奇心和 alignment 的讨论。Ronak 对比了 recursive self improvement（把模型放进黑箱自我优化）和 continual learning（从与人的交互中学习）。"我不想到达一个 agent 失控的世界，我认为做到这一点的方式就是坚持走 alignment 这条路。"他对缺失之处的表述是：LLM 作为 next token predictor 直接从状态零跳到状态一，但对 transition function 没有任何表示，而那是小孩扔一百次橄榄球才学会的东西。把这个差距量化出来，就有了好奇心。他对产品构建者未来的想象很美：当 harness 成为产品的大部分，构建者就变成园丁，立好棚架让植物自己生长，注入的是品味而不是代码。
